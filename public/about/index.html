About OccupationPay

OccupationPay is an online salary information platform designed to help people explore wages and employment estimates across occupations in the United States.

Our goal is to make occupational salary information easier to find, understand and compare. Whether you are planning a career, researching an occupation or comparing earning potential, OccupationPay provides a simple way to explore available data.

Our Data Sources

OccupationPay uses occupational wage estimates from the United States Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS), specifically its Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS) program.

The database currently uses May 2025 national occupational wage estimates.

The data source is publicly available from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics .

Salary Methodology

Our occupation pages display several wage measures, including mean annual wage, median annual wage, mean hourly wage and median hourly wage.

We also display selected salary percentiles, including the 10th, 25th, 75th and 90th percentiles. These figures help illustrate the distribution of reported wages within an occupation.

Employment estimates are included where available in the source data.

Understanding Salary Estimates

Salary figures shown on OccupationPay are statistical estimates, not guaranteed earnings or individual salary offers.

Actual wages can vary depending on geographic location, experience, education, employer, industry and other factors.

National estimates provide a general overview and should not be interpreted as a guarantee of what a particular person will earn.

Our Mission

We aim to make reliable public salary information more accessible and useful for career exploration and informed decision-making.

OccupationPay is an independent information platform and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics.
